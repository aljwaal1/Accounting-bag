# Accounting Bag — privacy draft

The app stores preferences, calculation history and saved feasibility projects locally on the device. Export and sharing are initiated by the user; receiving apps may handle exported data under their own policies. No account or remote storage is implemented in the current source. Device backups may include local data depending on Android settings. Removing app data deletes local records; exported copies must be removed separately.

Before publication, verify the final Android manifest, dependencies, backup configuration, actual network behavior and Google Play Data Safety declarations. Publish this notice at a public URL with developer contact information. This draft is not a completed Play compliance review.
